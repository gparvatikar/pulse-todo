Privacy Policy — Pulse ToDo

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

Pulse ToDo ("the App") is a task management application designed with privacy first. Your tasks, notes, categories, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • On-Device Storage: All tasks, due dates, recurrence schedules, and categories you create are stored directly on your device using local application storage or your self-hosted SQLite database.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or user profiling tools.
  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the App without creating an external account or providing personal contact information.

3. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location data.

4. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. Deleting a task or category inside the App permanently removes it from storage, and uninstalling the App removes all locally stored application data from your device.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact support via the store listing page.
